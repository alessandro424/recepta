Brand guidelines · v1.0

Recepta, in a nutshell.

Recepta comes from “recepción” — reception, the heart of every property. The brand should feel like arriving at a great hotel: warm, reliable and smart, with someone clearly looking after you.

Logo

The symbol merges a chat bubble with a reception bell: conversation and hospitality in a single mark. The wordmark is always set in lowercase.

Color

Violet is the accent: buttons, key numbers and the word that matters. Midnight brings tech credibility, Mist keeps pages light and calm, and Mint signals that something has been resolved.

Violet#6C4CF5 · action, accent

Midnight#14123B · text, dark surfaces

Mist#F5F4FB · main background

Mint#7EE8C3 · highlights on dark

Deep Mint#0E9F75 · success, “resolved”

Typography

Fraunces, a warm serif with editorial character, for headlines. Inter, neutral and highly legible, for everything else.

Headlines · Fraunces 600

Your front desk never sleeps.

Body · Inter 400 / 600

AI agents connected to your PMS that answer, resolve and upsell every guest. Instantly, in their language.

Voice & tone

We sound like a great head of reception: friendly, decisive and jargon-free.

✓ Do

  • “Your agents reply while you sleep.”
  • Short sentences, action verbs.
  • Concrete, verifiable numbers.
  • Talk to the reader as “you”.

✗ Don't

  • “Disruptive LLM-powered omnichannel solution.”
  • Promise that AI replaces the team.
  • Unexplained technical jargon.
  • Exclamation marks everywhere.
