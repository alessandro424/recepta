    The agents

    A team of specialist agents, not a generic chatbot.

    Each agent has one job, clear rules and scoped permissions in your PMS. They read the booking, act on it — and know exactly when to hand the conversation to a human.

    ✈️
    Pre-arrival

    Welcome Agent

    Reaches out days before arrival, confirms the arrival time, answers transport questions and collects special requests.

    → booking.arrival_time = "23:30"
    🪪
    Arrival

    Check-in Agent

    Nudges guests to complete online check-in, chases the ones who haven't, and sends access instructions or the smart-lock code once everything is in order.

    → lock.send_code(room 204)
    🛎️
    In-stay

    Concierge Agent

    Wi-Fi, parking, restaurants, breakfast hours or how the induction hob works. Answers from your house guide and local recommendations.

    → guide.search("nearby parking")
    💶
    Revenue

    Upsell Agent

    Offers early check-in, late check-out, room upgrades or transfers at the right moment — with real prices and availability — and charges on the spot.

    → pms.add_charge("late_checkout", €25)
    🧰
    Operations

    Issues Agent

    Spots complaints and breakdowns, opens the task for housekeeping or maintenance, and pings the manager on duty when it's urgent.

    → task.create(maintenance, priority: high)
    ⭐
    Post-stay

    Reviews Agent

    Asks how the stay went before guests say it in public, invites happy guests to leave a review and drafts replies to the ones already posted.

    → ota.reply_review(draft)

    FAQ

    What people ask before getting started.

    Does it work with my PMS?

    Recepta integrates with the most widely used PMSs and channel managers for hotels and vacation rentals. If yours has an API, we'll connect it — and if it's not on the list yet, we build the connector during onboarding at no cost on the Growth plan.

    What if the agent doesn't know the answer?

    It won't make one up. When a question isn't covered by your information, or a guest is upset, the agent escalates to your team with a summary and a suggested reply.

    Can I review replies before they go out?

    Yes. Every agent starts in draft mode. You decide which message types, channels or agents move to autopilot, and when.

    What about my guests' data?

    Everything is hosted in the European Union, we're GDPR-compliant and we sign a data processing agreement with every customer. We never use your conversations to train third-party models.

    How long does setup take?

    Connecting the PMS takes minutes. Full onboarding — house guide, tone and rules — is usually done in under a week.
